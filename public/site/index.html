About Us

About On Point Electrical

Founded in 2022, On Point Electrical was built on a simple belief — that every home and business deserves safe, reliable, and professional electrical work. Based in Limpopo and proudly serving both Limpopo and Gauteng, we've grown from the ground up by letting our workmanship speak for itself. From small repairs to full installations, our team brings skill, honesty, and attention to detail to every job. We don't cut corners — we connect them. Whether you're upgrading your lighting, fixing a fault, or wiring a new space, you can count on us to show up on time, do the job right, and leave the place better than we found it.
